Privacy Policy

Coachmaxxing · Effective October 28, 2026

The short version
  • You can use Coachmaxxing without an account. Your log then stays on your iPhone.
  • Physique photos and everything in Protocols (medications, doses, phases) never leave your iPhone.
  • If you sign in, your log syncs to our database so it's backed up and works across devices.
  • Meal photos and coach questions are sent to our AI provider to answer them. We don't keep the photos.
  • No ads, no tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Physique photos. Stored in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded or sent to the AI.
  • Protocols. Medications, injections, doses, sites, supply and phases are stored on the device only, are never synced, and are never sent to the AI. The AI coach only learns the training intensity you choose for a phase ("Push hard", "Steady" or "Ease off") and which week of it you're in, never its name or what you take.
  • Your coach chat history. Saved on the device. Each message you send is passed to the AI to get a reply (see below).
  • Reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. We don't use push notification servers.

What syncs if you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google creates an account so your data is backed up. We receive the email address Apple or Google shares with us (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). We then store:

  • Your profile: name or nickname, sex, age, height, weight, training experience, goal and pace, activity level, eating style, allergies, injuries or limits, and the "why" you wrote, if any.
  • Your answer to the setup question about eating and food, which only changes how the coach works with you. It is never shared or used for marketing.
  • Your logs: meals and their nutrition, saved meals, recipes and custom foods, weigh-ins, body measurements, training plans, sessions and personal records, calorie targets and check-in history.
  • How many AI requests you've made each day, to enforce the free limit, and your subscription status.

AI requests require an account and go through our server, so your AI provider never receives your email. Anthropic processes requests under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and our server (United States).
  • Anthropic: AI meal estimates, coaching and plans.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions using an anonymous ID. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in. Apple also delivers the app, TestFlight builds and any crash reports or feedback you choose to send.
  • Open Food Facts: packaged food and barcode lookups.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: You → Erase everything on this device deletes your log, Protocols and physique photos from the device. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Your account: email gonzalezaidan106@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and everything synced to it within 30 days.

Age and safety

Coachmaxxing is for people 13 and older. Under 18, the app does not offer calorie deficits, contest prep, physique reads or Protocols. Coachmaxxing gives estimates for general fitness, not medical advice, and never recommends medications, drugs or doses.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Synced data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only read its own records. The AI provider key stays on our server and is never in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll tell you in the app.
